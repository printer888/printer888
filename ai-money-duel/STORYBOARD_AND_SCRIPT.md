## Accuracy notes
* Claude is shown as an **analysis assistant**. The prompt asks it not to assume profits are guaranteed, its answer says nothing is guaranteed, and the human makes the decision ("Claude analysed · Dmitri decides").
* All trading is labelled on screen: **"SIMULATED TRADING · FICTIONAL RESULTS · NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE"**. The +£450 and +£2,850 figures are story props.
* The Claude interface is a stylised recreation: sidebar with New chat, Chats and Projects; the "+" attach button; file chip; send button.

## How the animation works (what is and isn't "3D")
* **Style:** 2.5D vector character animation rendered frame by frame in a headless browser. Head turns shift the features and ears, and multi‑layer parallax plus depth‑of‑field blur give the camera depth. It is **not** photoreal 3D. No 3D character assets or AI video generators were available in the build environment.
* **Lip‑sync:** taken from the TTS model's own phoneme durations (25 ms resolution), converted to visemes with co‑articulation blending. Only the speaking character's mouth moves; listeners blink, look at the speaker, nod and react.
