## Fact check (support.claude.com / claude.com, Nov 2026)
* The "+" button at the lower left of the chat box adds files or photos.
* **Projects** are available on every plan. Free accounts can create up to 5. Projects hold shared knowledge and instructions.
* **Artifacts** are available on every plan, including Free. They need the "Create and edit files" capability enabled in Settings.
* Editing a sent message creates a new response branch, with ‹ › to switch versions.
* Asking Claude to ask clarifying questions first is a prompting technique, not a button.
* The Claude screens are stylised recreations, not screen recordings.
